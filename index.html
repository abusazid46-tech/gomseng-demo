About GOMSENG

Rooted in Assam, styled for modern celebrations.

GOMSENG is presented as a Jorhat-based Assamese silk and jewellery boutique for customers who want cultural clothing to feel personal, premium and easy to buy. The store experience centers on Mekhela Chador, Muga-inspired palettes, Pat silk, Axomiya Gohona and complete occasion styling.

This demo frames the business as a trusted local destination where families can select wedding pieces, festival wear and gifting looks with clear product guidance and fast WhatsApp support.

Mission

To make authentic Assamese occasion wear easier to discover, style and purchase.

GOMSENG can win online by giving every customer clarity: fabric type, price range, jewellery pairing, delivery options and a direct WhatsApp path to the store.

Vision

To become a loved Assamese silk destination for brides, families and the diaspora.

The brand can grow beyond Jorhat by combining cultural trust with polished digital catalogues, social proof and warm remote-shopping support.
